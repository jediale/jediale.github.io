UF-WHOA

Balance toy final project for MIT Toy Product Design class, 2.00b, designed by the six memebrs of Team Polar Bear.

Date: Spring 2023 Course: MIT 2.00b: Toy Product Design Tools: CNC, Laser Cutter, Arduino, Adobe Illustrator

Overview

UF-WHOA is a circular, alien-themed balance board game that challenges players to step on illuminated buttons and twist on the board as prompted. I was a member of Team Polar Bear, a six member squad tasked to conceptualize, design, test, and present a final prototype for a brand new toy over the course of a semester. We presented "UF-WHOA" in-person and live online (see video below). I did ergonomics research to determine dimensions and materials for the board; ultimately, we settled on a polyurethane chassis with a flexible PET-G sheet to form electrical contacts when pressed. From there, I created the initial sketches for the toy and a diagram for CNC fabrication, as well as a circular grip tape pattern. (I am also the voice of the toy!) For this project, we applied design techniques and shop skills that we learned in class. We also conducted research by presenting our prototypes to kids for real feedback and iteration.
